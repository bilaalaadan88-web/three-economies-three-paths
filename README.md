# Three Economies, Three Paths: USA, India & Japan

An interactive R Shiny dashboard comparing inflation, real interest rates, 
and unemployment across three structurally different economies from 1991–2021.

## Features
- Inflation, interest rate, and unemployment trend lines
- Phillips Curve scatter plot (unemployment vs inflation)
- Inflation volatility comparison across countries
- Summary statistics table (averages, extremes, volatility)

## Key Findings
- India shows the highest average inflation (7.24%) and by far the 
  highest volatility (3.24 std. dev.)
- Japan shows near-zero average inflation (0.35%), even dipping into 
  deflation, reflecting decades of economic stagnation
- The United States sits closest to the ~2% inflation target most 
  central banks aim for, with the lowest volatility
